About Me

Analytical thinking. Real-world questions.

I am a Cal Poly Humboldt student pursuing Data Science with Applied Mathematics and Economics with Applied Mathematics. My academic work combines computational analysis with an interest in how environmental, economic, and public systems affect communities.

Through research experiences spanning methane emissions, wildfire risk, and community resilience, I have worked with heterogeneous datasets and quantitative methods to investigate problems that require both technical rigor and contextual understanding.

I am particularly interested in using data science to evaluate policy, understand systemic risk, and help develop solutions that are both analytically sound and socially meaningful.

My approach is grounded in a simple principle: good analysis should make complex problems easier to understand, evaluate, and address.
